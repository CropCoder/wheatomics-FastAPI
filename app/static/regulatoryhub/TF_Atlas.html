Data citation

Ren K., Wang Y., Cui Y., et al. (2026). Construction and validation of a monoclonal yeast transcription factor library for high-throughput interaction screening in wheat. Journal of Integrative Agriculture, 25(10), 4378–4382. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jia.2026.07.037.

Motif data: promoter motif scanning used the plant position frequency matrices (PFMs) from Download PFM data for plants in JASPAR CORE, JASPAR (https://jaspar.elixir.no/).

Pfam domain data: Zheng Y., Jiao C., Sun H., et al. (2016). iTAK: a program for genome-wide prediction and classification of plant transcription factors, transcriptional regulators, and protein kinases. Molecular Plant, 9(12), 1667–1670. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molp.2016.09.014.

TF classifications are candidate annotations; motif hits and variant overlaps do not prove TF–DNA binding.
